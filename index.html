01 · Intelligence

One score. Three signals.

The Pulse Engine turns raw job rows into a transparent 0–100 hiring signal. No black-box claim: every component is inspectable.

Share of postings exposing compensation information. A market-data signal, not a maturity claim.

Share of postings detected within the last 30 days.
